Harun Khan

PhD student in Computer Science, Stanford University

I work on formal verification: making automated reasoning tools trustworthy by producing proofs that can be checked in the Lean theorem prover. I'm a member of the Stanford Center for Automated Reasoning (Centaur), advised by Prof. Clark Barrett. My recent work connects the SMT solver cvc5 to Lean, including verified bit-blasting and proof rules for nonlinear and transcendental arithmetic.

Before Stanford I studied mathematics at Imperial College London, where I started formalizing mathematics in Lean with Prof. Kevin Buzzard. Prior to that I won a bronze medal at the International Mathematical Olympiad.

Outside Research

I raced slalom and giant slalom on the Stanford Ski Racing Team. On the adventurous side, I ski off cliffs and cliff dive into lakes. On the quieter side, I find sustained focus and flow in climbing and in puzzle games like Simon Tatham's collection and Snakebird.

I enjoy coaching math olympiad students and writing problems. I trained Pakistan's IMO team, and wrote problems for and later chaired the Imperial College Mathematics Competition.

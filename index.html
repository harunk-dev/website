Harun Khan

Formal verification researcher · Stanford CS PhD

I work on formal verification in Lean, advised by Prof. Clark Barrett in the Stanford Center for Automated Reasoning. Most of my work bridges SMT solvers and interactive theorem provers: connecting cvc5 to Lean so that automated solvers produce proofs Lean can check. More recently I've worked on using AI to discover formally verified quantum programs at Google DeepMind, and I'm excited to bring this kind of algorithmic discovery to theoretical computer science.

Before Stanford I studied mathematics at Imperial College London, where I started formalizing mathematics in Lean with Prof. Kevin Buzzard. Prior to that I won a bronze medal at the International Mathematical Olympiad.

Outside Research

I spend as much of my free time as I can in nature. After all, nature is God's theater. On the adventurous side, I raced slalom and giant slalom on the Stanford Ski Racing Team. I also ski off cliffs and cliff dive into alpine lakes. On quieter days I find flow in climbing.

Indoors, I love puzzles like Snakebird, coaching math olympiad students and writing problems. I trained Pakistan's IMO team one summer, and wrote problems for and later chaired the Imperial College Mathematics Competition.

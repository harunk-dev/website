Harun Khan

PhD student in Computer Science, Stanford University

I work on formal verification: making automated reasoning tools trustworthy by producing proofs that can be checked in the Lean theorem prover. I'm a member of the Stanford Center for Automated Reasoning (Centaur), advised by Prof. Clark Barrett. My recent work connects the SMT solver cvc5 to Lean, including verified bit-blasting and proof rules for nonlinear and transcendental arithmetic.

Before Stanford I studied mathematics at Imperial College London, where I started formalizing mathematics in Lean with Prof. Kevin Buzzard. I'm an IMO medalist, and I've trained Pakistan's IMO team.
